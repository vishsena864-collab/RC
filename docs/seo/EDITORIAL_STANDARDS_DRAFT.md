# ChatSpark editorial standards — internal draft, not a published policy

**Proposed:** 2026-10-31. **Status:** DRAFT — awaiting operator, editorial and legal sign-off.

## Mission and audience
ChatSpark publishes helpful information for adults 18+ who want text-only random chats, shared music rooms, online graffiti, and poetry challenges. Material must remain useful without relying on ranking manipulation. We publish neither private conversations nor visitor artwork without explicit permission.

## Accountability and provenance
Before launching a guide, record a real responsible editor, a factual reviewer when appropriate, an evidence log, actual publication and revision dates, and a monitored correction channel. Never invent real-person credentials, customer quotes, live user counts, engagement figures, audited security claims, test results, or online ratings. A truthful collective/team byline is acceptable only when it accurately identifies the accountable publisher.

## How claims are verified
- Product functionality: reproduce the exact flow on the current production version with at least two consenting test browsers where relevant; log the date, environment and any errors. Source code shows intended design, not uptime.
- Privacy and safety: compare web/lib/legal.js, server/src/privacy.js, server/src/moderation.js, live settings and providers before any strong privacy or moderation promise. Obtain qualified legal/privacy review before publication.
- Comparative content: use a dated feature matrix for each tested provider. Separate verified facts from uncertain, changed or unavailable features. State any relationship or conflicts of interest.
- Original examples: use synthetic examples clearly labeled as such, or publish only permission-cleared, anonymized examples. Do not show identifiable or private user messages.
- Content drafting: automated assistance is permitted for drafts and formatting, but a human must add original evidence, confirm facts and perform final review. Disclose substantial automation when readers would reasonably expect it.
- SEO: one useful canonical page per specific intent; no doorway pages, spun translations, scraped content or mass-generated template pages.

## Editorial release gates (all applicable must pass)
1. Named responsible operator/team and working support or corrections contact.
2. Verified claim ledger and references; first-hand evidence for any empirical test.
3. Human editorial review; legal/safety review when sensitive.
4. Accurate screenshots and alt text; license/consent recorded.
5. Actual page H1/title/canonical and relevant JSON-LD reflect user-visible facts.
6. Working mobile experience, prominent 18+ policy and CTA.
7. Recorded date of meaningful publication or change; test staging and production.
8. Final owner approval before deployment.

**Corrections:** Log error report, triage risk, correct harmful inaccuracies, record revision, and make material corrections clear to readers. Do not silently alter dates just to appear fresh.

## Public-facing draft for later approval
**How ChatSpark checks what it publishes**

We aim to publish original, useful guidance about anonymous text chat, music rooms and creative challenges. Product guides are based on documented features and hands-on testing. We identify a real responsible editor or team, review important privacy and safety claims, and show when information has been materially updated.

When comparing other websites, we use an explicitly dated checklist and explain limitations. We do not fabricate rankings, users, testimonials or performance results. We correct factual errors and welcome reports through **[VERIFIED, MONITORED CONTACT]**.

ChatSpark is intended for adults 18+. Anonymous does not mean private from internet providers or hosting services, and interactions with strangers carry risks. We do not publish private messages or visitor artwork without permission.
